How it works

From first call to monthly close

  1. Consultation

    We talk through your business, the accounts you use, and roughly how many transactions you have each month.

  2. Setup and catch-up

    If you need it, we set up QuickBooks Online and your chart of accounts, and bring any past months up to date.

  3. Monthly bookkeeping

    Each month we categorize your transactions, reconcile your accounts, and close the books.

  4. Reports and review

    You receive your financial statements. Growth and Complete clients also meet with us each month to go over them.
